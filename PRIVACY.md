# Privacy Policy for RootMusic Bot

**Last Updated:** September 24, 2026

RootMusic ("we", "our", or "the bot") operates as a music playback service for communities on the Root platform (`rootapp.com`).

---

## 1. Information Processed

RootMusic processes only the minimal transient data required for playback:
- **Requested Song Query:** User query strings (YouTube titles or URLs) to search and retrieve media streams.
- **User Identifier:** Root User GUID (`userId`) associated with song requests for display in the queue.
- **Community Channel:** Root Channel GUID where the music player was initiated.

RootMusic **does not** retain personal user data, store chat logs, or monetize user information.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services

Audio searches and metadata are retrieved via public APIs (including YouTube and web media streams). Interactions with third-party audio streams are subject to their respective terms of service.

---

## 3. Contact

For inquiries or support regarding RootMusic, join our developer channel or visit our official GitHub repository.
